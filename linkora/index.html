PRIVACY

Browser-first

LINKORA does not ask for platform passwords or upload your file to our server.

Platform compatibility: Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, X, YouTube and other social/video services do not provide a universal public download endpoint. LINKORA does not bypass logins, private-content controls, DRM, paywalls or platform restrictions. Use each service's official download/export feature, or paste a direct file link that its publisher makes available.

Responsible downloading

Only download content you created, own, have permission to save, or that the publisher expressly offers for download. Respect copyright, privacy, platform terms and local law. Never enter social-media passwords, session cookies or private access tokens into a downloader. LINKORA is an independent Toolwise Daily project and is not affiliated with the platforms mentioned.

Frequently asked questions

Why does a social-media link not download?

Social platforms often require authentication, signed URLs or their own download tools. LINKORA intentionally does not extract protected content or work around platform restrictions.

Why can't LINKORA check some direct links?

A source may block cross-origin browser requests. If the source is public and you trust it, you can open the original link and use its own download option.

Is LINKORA free?

Yes. This version is free to use. Your browser and the file host may apply their own limits.

Are my links stored?

This page does not send pasted links to a LINKORA server or save a link history. Your browser still communicates with the website you choose to open.
