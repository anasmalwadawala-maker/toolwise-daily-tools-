PRIVACY

Temporary processing

Engine downloads are held temporarily on the backend and may be cleared after a short period or when a free server restarts.

YouTube owner downloads: LINKORA does not extract video streams from YouTube. For a video uploaded to your own channel, use YouTube Studio or Google Takeout to download the official MP4/export. YouTube says its Studio download is generally 720p or 360p depending on the video. For someone else’s upload, ask the creator to provide the original file or a direct download link. LINKORA never bypasses sign-in, DRM, private-content controls or platform restrictions.

Responsible downloading

Only download content you created, own, have permission to save, or that the publisher expressly offers for download. Respect copyright, privacy, platform terms and local law. Never enter social-media passwords, session cookies or private access tokens into a downloader. LINKORA is an independent Toolwise Daily project and is not affiliated with the platforms mentioned.

Frequently asked questions

Can LINKORA download my YouTube uploads?

LINKORA detects YouTube links and guides you to YouTube Studio or Google Takeout. You must sign in to the account that owns the channel and use YouTube’s own download/export controls. LINKORA does not fetch YouTube video streams.

Can I download someone else’s YouTube video if they gave permission?

YouTube’s help page says you cannot download other users’ videos through its standard download feature. Ask the creator to send you the original file or provide a direct file link they are authorized to share.

Why does another social-media link not download?

Social platforms often require authentication, signed URLs or their own download tools. LINKORA intentionally does not extract protected content or work around platform restrictions.

Why can't LINKORA check some direct links?

A source may deny server-side retrieval, require sign-in, rate-limit requests or block automated access. If you have permission, use the publisher’s own download option.

Is LINKORA free?

Yes. This version is free to use. Your browser and the file host may apply their own limits.

Are my links stored?

Direct-file requests are proxied through Cloudflare and are not saved by the direct-file function. Supported media-page requests are sent to the separate download engine; its queue is held in memory and downloaded files are temporarily stored on that server. Temporary files may be cleared after the retention window or when the host restarts. Do not submit private links, credentials or sensitive URLs.
