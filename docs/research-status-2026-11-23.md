## Current public SEO policy guardrail

Google's current spam policies explicitly identify scaled content abuse as mass-producing unoriginal pages with little user value, including large volumes of AI-generated pages without added value. Google also lists misleading functionality, such as pages promising a tool that does not actually work. Toolwise Daily must publish only tested tools and useful pages with distinct user value.
